About The Real Record Store

Independent record shop founded in Seattle 1992 by seven friends. This is the true story, of seven strangers, picked to live in a city, work together, and have their lives taped. Find out what happens, when people stop being polite, and start getting real in a record store. ( JK) But we are real people who love records! We bring together a curated selection of new and used vinyl, local artists, and community events. Our mission is to foster a love for music and support the local music scene. We carry all types of records to reach all audiences.

Come by and check us out. If we don't have it in stock we will order it for you. Just ask!
